05 / A LITTLE CLARITY

Good questions.
Straight answers.

How much of my time do you need?+

Our production model is built around two dedicated days per month. We prepare the research and scripts, guide you on camera, and handle editing and publishing.

I’ve never been on camera. Is that okay?+

Absolutely. You get on-camera direction, prompting, and time to find your rhythm. You bring your expertise; we help you communicate it naturally.

What are the monthly packages?+

Basic is ₹30,000/month for 10–12 reels. Premium is ₹45,000/month with a two-day shoot and 20–24 videos. Advanced is ₹60,000/month with 30 reels, long-form content, and paid retargeting. We’ll confirm scope and fit on your discovery call.

How do you handle medical content?+

We develop content around your expertise and send scripts and videos for your review. Nothing is filmed or published without your approval.

Where do you work?+

We’re based in Hyderabad, with on-site shoots across Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, and major cities in India. Contact us to discuss your location and production needs.
